# blocklist

A curated collection of domain blocklists used with AdGuard Home to block ads, trackers, telemetry, and unwanted content across your network.

## Overview

This repository contains custom and aggregated domain blocklists that I use to enhance privacy and reduce unwanted ads and tracking on all devices connected to my home network via AdGuard Home.

The lists include domains related to:

- Advertising networks
- Analytics and telemetry services
- Social media trackers
- Malicious or suspicious domains

## Usage

To use these blocklists with AdGuard Home:

1. Clone or download this repository.
2. Add the blocklist URLs or files to your AdGuard Home configuration under **Filters**.
3. Update your filters regularly to keep blocking new trackers and ads.

## Why Use Custom Blocklists?

- **Privacy:** Blocks domains that collect data about your browsing habits.
- **Performance:** Reduces bandwidth and speeds up page loading by blocking unwanted content.
- **Control:** Customize which domains to block or allow on your network.
